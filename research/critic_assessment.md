# Completeness critic (discovery round 1)

The 429-entry roster is strong on research disciplines: complexity, maths, biology, neuro, consciousness, deliberation, economics, safety science and peace. It has five systematic blind spots. (1) Organisation type: it is built from labs, NGOs and funders, and under-covers membership bodies (unions, learned and professional societies, standards bodies, national ethics councils), which carry practice standards and mass reach. (2) int/a's own home milieu, the integral, contemplative-retreat, flourishing-science and wisdom-culture institutions, is mostly missing, even though many of them (Wisdom 2.0, Esalen, CCARE, UVA CSC, Harvard HFP, Pocket Project) launched AI programming in 2025-26. (3) Faith coverage follows the Catholic/WCC and FLI-grantee tracks plus Western Buddhism, and misses interreligious machinery (G20 Interfaith Forum, KAICIID, Religions for Peace) along with Jewish, evangelical, Asian engaged-Buddhist and Baha'i bodies. (4) Futures studies as a discipline is absent: the Millennium Project's AGI governance work is a clear bridge-ring miss. (5) Relational AI-minds work outside the EA welfare cluster is missing, the clearest single miss being Anima Labs. Geography also skews Anglophone and is thin in Berlin and Paris, where int/a has hubs, as well as in Japan's ELSI ecosystem, India beyond Aapti, and Latin America.

How this was checked: the session's WebSearch budget was exhausted (200/200), so I ran no searches. I checked each named organisation by fetching its own site directly, about 150 pages through a parallel probe plus WebFetch. Domains that did not resolve or returned 403, left as unverified leads: Bantucracy (Sabelo Mhlambi, Ubuntu ethics), HKU AI & Humanity Lab, Center for Long-term AI, the Baha'i International Community and SAG-AFTRA. Checked and showing no AI work (useful negatives): Fetzer, Kalliopeia, 1440, Templeton Religion Trust, SOIF, the Future Generations Commissioner for Wales, Stockholm's Institute for Futures Studies, Center for Healthy Minds, Emory's CBCT centre, CIIS, Metaphorum, Academy for Systems Change and the Golden Sufi Center (no current AI work).
